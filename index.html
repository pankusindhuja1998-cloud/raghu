01 // ARCHITECTURE & MECHANISMS

Four Pillars of Speech Emotion Distillation

Why naive pruning fails on prosody—and how multi-level knowledge transfer preserves emotional intelligence under extreme compression.

01

Response-Based (Logit) KD

A temperature-scaled softmax (τ > 1) exposes the teacher's dark knowledge—revealing secondary affective overlap such as Angry ↔ Aroused or Sad ↔ Neutral rather than brittle one-hot labels.

L_KL = τ² · D_KL( σ(z_T/τ) ∥ σ(z_S/τ) )
02

Hidden-State & Attention Transfer

Aligns intermediate transformer representations using cosine similarity and L2 projection heads. Early layers transfer acoustic frame structure; deeper layers transfer speaker-invariant emotional cues.

L_feat = Σ || W_proj(h_S^l) − h_T^m ||²
03

Acoustic-Prosodic Feature KD

Bridges raw-waveform Self-Supervised Teachers (Wav2Vec 2.0 / WavLM) with lightweight Mel-Spectrogram + F0/MFCC student CNNs, removing heavy convolutional feature encoders at inference time.

Raw Audio (Teacher) → Log-Mel + F0 (Student)
04

Relational & Contrastive KD

Preserves pairwise distances and angular geometry in the embedding space across utterances, ensuring the compressed student separates subtle valence/arousal clusters under noisy edge microphones.

L_RKD = ψ_dist(e_i, e_j) + ψ_angle(e_i, e_j, e_k)
